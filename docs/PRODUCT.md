# Product

## Promise

*Sound the way you want to sound.* Shadow a British RP model every day, see exactly what to fix, and watch your accent match improve.

## Who it's for

Any learner who wants a clearer, more natural British accent. IELTS Speaking candidates are the first audience (the built-in library and the interview are IELTS-style), but the method and the app are not exam-specific.

## The core loop

| Step | What the learner does | What the app does |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Listen | Hears the script read by a British RP model | Plays a cached Gemini TTS clip at 0.75x, 0.85x or 1x; marks stressed words |
| Shadow | Repeats each breath group straight after the model | Plays a phrase, opens the mic for about as long, keeps each repeat for A/B |
| Deliver | Says the whole answer | Sends model clip + take to the coach; returns accent scores, one focus tip, mistakes with sound/stress tips, and a before/after against the last take |

Repetition is the point: learners are encouraged to repeat the same script many times rather than chase new content.

## Screens

- **Today**: first-run welcome (method, accent, starting pace, optional name), then a daily suggested script, streak and recent reps.
- **Library**: the IELTS Speaking pack and the learner's own scripts, with reps and best score.
- **Practice**: the loop above, one script per URL.
- **Progress**: streak, weekly average, weakest accent area with advice, score trend, practice calendar, capture history.
- **Capture**: a short live interview (Parts 1–3) whose answers become personal scripts; unfinished interviews are saved; the report lets the learner choose which scripts to keep and shows a band report.
- **Settings**: model voice and speed, profile, theme, backup, restore and erase.

## Principles

- **Honest feedback.** Accent scores are the AI coach's judgement from listening to both clips and are labelled as guidance. The interview band report is graded from text, so its pronunciation score is marked as an estimate.
- **The learner's own words.** Personal scripts only use what the learner actually said; nothing is invented, and nothing they have is overwritten.
- **Local-first and private.** No accounts. Data stays in the browser; only audio for coaching and text for grading is sent to Gemini, and recordings are never stored.
- **Respect attention.** No pop-ups on first run, clear next steps, keyboard and screen-reader friendly, light and dark themes.

## Content

- 12 original Cambridge-style model answers (Parts 1–3) written for this app.
- Cambridge-style interview question banks. They are written in the style of the test, not taken from published books.

## Known limits

- Accent scoring is an LLM's listening judgement, not phoneme-level acoustic analysis.
- The model voice is a prebuilt Gemini voice prompted for a British accent, not a recording of a specific RP speaker.
- Browser storage only: clearing site data removes progress unless a backup was downloaded.
